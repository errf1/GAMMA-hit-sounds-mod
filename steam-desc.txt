[h1]GAMMA Hit Sounds[/h1]
Bring the impact sounds of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. G.A.M.M.A. to RimWorld. Firearm injuries use randomized clips matched to the struck body part and its armor.

[img]https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c3d2fbd-b733-47fc-8565-0db5f589b547[/img]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Separate randomized pools for armored and unarmored body hits, helmet hits, and unhelmeted head hits.
[*]The unhelmeted head pool combines gas-mask and unarmored sounds.
[*]Limits regular hit sounds to one per pawn per game tick.
[*]Bullet-related armor deflections use mechanoid impact sounds when no regular injury sound played.
[*]Suppresses the matching vanilla ground-impact sound to avoid doubled effects.
[*]Direct mechanoid injury sounds remain handled by the base game or other mods.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]
[*][b]Harmony required.[/b]
[*][b]Combat Extended is optional.[/b] When installed, selected CE bullet-like damage types and partial apparel armor values are supported.
[/list]
