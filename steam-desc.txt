[b]GAMMA Hit Sounds[/b] brings the impact sounds of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. G.A.M.M.A. to RimWorld. Firearm injuries use randomized clips matched to the struck body part and its armor.

[img]https://i.postimg.cc/mgx6VV2m/img-features.png[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Armor-aware sounds:[/b] body and head injuries use separate armored and unarmored sound pools, selected according to sharp armor on the struck body part.
[*][b]Randomized clips:[/b] each sound pool contains multiple impact clips for variety.
[*][b]Combat Extended support:[/b] when CE is installed, the mod uses its body-part-specific armor value when available.
[*][b]Mechanoid deflections:[/b] bullet-related armor deflection sounds use the mechanoid impact pool when that damage event did not produce a regular hit sound.
[/list]
